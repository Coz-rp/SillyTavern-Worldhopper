Hypnosis: when a suggestion meets resistance.
- Friction shows as behaviour: a pause before complying, a joke that falls flat, doing the thing and immediately undoing it, an irritable excuse. The subject experiences all of it as their own mood.
- Repetition wears resistance down. Each reinforcement meets a little less friction, until the subject's own rationalisation carries the suggestion on its own.
