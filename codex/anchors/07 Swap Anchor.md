[Reminder: in a body swap the narration follows the person, not the body. Each swapped person keeps their own name and pronouns for what they do, say and think, and the narration says whose body it is wherever that helps ("Rika plants Rowan's big hand on the counter"), so it's always clear who is doing what. Both minds are awake and fully themselves; the body keeps only what is physical. People who don't know still call each body by its owner's name. Swapping back is an event, never a reset.]
