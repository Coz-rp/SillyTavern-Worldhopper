Body Ledger
Keeps track of who is in which body (possession, multipossession, skinsuits, hive minds, copies, swaps) and who has which power. It updates after each reply in chats with one of those modes, and goes into the prompt so names, pronouns and every change stay straight. Each body someone takes over gets three short lines on who its owner is in their own life (look, voice and habits, life), written once by your notes model, so the people around it keep treating it as who it was, and the owner is themselves again once released. teal rows are yours; violet rows are someone else's. Mind changes like hypnosis and mind control belong to the Mind Book, so the Ledger only comes in with a body mode, and it shows here only then. After each reply it reads only the newest messages, never the whole chat. While you run more than one body, a card per body sits over the message box: tap one to start a line like [Kayla] for that body. A line with no name is you as a whole, and the writer only ever does what you wrote with your bodies.

Mind Book
Keeps what Hypnosis, Mind Control and Altered Perception have put into people, each in its own record: a hypnotized person's trance stage, sessions, suggestions and triggers; what a controller has set in someone's will; who can't perceive what. It updates only on replies that touch those things, and goes into the prompt with a line for the moment when it matters: a trigger word just said, a controlled person in the scene, someone who can't see a person who is right there.

Models
Worldhopper's background jobs run through your connection profiles (plug menu → Connection Profile), separate from the model you chat with. Give them their own model: a local one (KoboldCpp, Ollama…) or a cheap, fast API model (the Flash, Haiku or mini tier). The jobs are small and the Ledger runs after every reply, so that keeps things quick and costs next to nothing; your big roleplay model would work, but at big-model prices on every reply, and your next reply waits for the Ledger. A job with no model picked simply doesn't run.
